Aa

A is for apple

/a/ — short ‘a’, mouth open wide like taking a big bite of an apple

Teacher Notes

🎬 Action

Mime holding a big juicy apple and taking a big crunchy bite saying /a/! /a/! Or wiggle fingers up your forearm like ants crawling.

🧩 ASD Tip

For tactile-sensitive children, demonstrate the ant crawling on your own arm or let them tap fingers on a table. A plastic toy apple gives a concrete visual and tactile anchor.
